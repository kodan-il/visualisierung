### Design Choice

#### Which time-based visualization did you choose?
Line Graphs

#### Why did you choose this visualization?
The usage of Line Chart as our continuity visualization is to ease up the user. This is influenced by how easy we get to see the data from time to time.

Other thatn that, with a lot of amount of STATION_ID, Line Graphs is more helpful and less confusing for user to read the data. With no overlapping colour, it is simpler for user to see the data served in the visualization
