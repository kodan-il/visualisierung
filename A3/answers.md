### Answers to the questions

#### 1. In which year and which weather station the lowest temperatures have been measured?
In 2020 at Station Feldberg

#### 2. At which stations the air pressure is lowest? Are there seasonal changes in air pressure?
At Station Feldberg. Based on the seasonal change, there is a change in air pressure, where the colder months gives a lower air pressure

#### 3. Is there some kind of relation between temperature and humidity?
Yes, lower temperature gives more humidity, while higher temperature gives less humidity. It can be seen the difference between summer and winter
