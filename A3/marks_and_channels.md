### Marks and Channels

#### Parallel Coordinates
- Marks: Lines that connect between axis
- Channels:  
    a. Color code -> Station ID  
    b. X1-> Sunshine data  
    c. X2-> Snow depth  
    d. X3-> Air Pressure  
    e. X4-> Air Temp  
    f. X5-> Humidity  
    g. X6-> Max Air Temp  
    h. X7-> Min Air Temp  

#### Your Chosen Time Visualization
- Marks: Points (Points are connected by the lines)
- Channels:  
    a. X-> Year and Months  
    b. Y-> Temperature Air  
    c. Color code -> Station ID
