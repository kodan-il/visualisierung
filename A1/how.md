### Task 3: How

#### Part 1: Explain your design decision

We use Bar Chart to visualize the question of "How many of each penguins species live on which of the three islands?" because it is one of the methods that is very easy to understand and very common to use. Depending on the viewers interest, they are able to read the exact populations number, but also understand the differences in the populations between the three species as well as the three islands.  On top of that, Bar chart helps the scientist to analyze the data directly with no requirement to do a repetitive checks towards the values.

The addition of the label on top of every bars intended to assure the clarity of the information, so that the scientist able to directly read and understand the data without guessing how tall are the bars of the chart.
